What we do

Full-Service Welding & Fabrication

From a single repair to a complete system, our craftsmen handle the whole job — concept, drawings, fabrication, and installation.

Stainless Fabrication

Our specialty. Custom stainless work for processing and manufacturing plants, built to hold up in washdown and food-grade environments.

Installation & Rigging

Setting, moving, and installing equipment safely — rigging for industrial, commercial, and residential projects.

Repair & Maintenance

Fast turnaround on custom metal item repairs and machining for repair items — keeping your line running.

Design & Engineering

Concept designs, fabrication and production drawings, and 3D parametric modeling — with licensed engineering support.

Machining

In-house machining for small and medium production runs, one-off parts, and hard-to-source replacements.

Portable Welding

On-site welding when the work can't come to the shop — stainless, aluminum, and carbon steel in the field.

Who we are

A Family Shop, Built on Its Word

Swanson's Welding & Fabrication is a full-service welding and fabrication company in Springdale, Arkansas, serving Northwest Arkansas and beyond.

With over 30 years of combined experience in the metal fabrication industry, the shop has been built on integrity, quality, and reliability. Our craftsmen are equipped to handle both small and large jobs, and we take the same care with a single repair as we do with a full system build.
