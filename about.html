Our story

From repair shop to multi-industry manufacturer

Swansons Welding and Fabrication, Inc. (a Swansons Welding LLC Company) started out in 2009 as a welding and repair company serving the poultry industry. It has since grown into a multi-industry manufacturing, fabrication and repair services company.

With the transition in ownership in 2021, Swansons added fully licensed Professional Engineering capabilities and invested in new state-of-the-art manufacturing equipment. Located in Springdale, Arkansas, our team brings decades of combined experience in metal fabrication and repair, and consistently delivers results for our customers.
