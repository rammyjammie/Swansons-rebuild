About Us

A family-owned welding and fabrication shop serving Northwest Arkansas and beyond.

Our story

Built on Integrity, Quality & Reliability

Swanson's Welding & Fabrication, Inc. is a full-service welding and fabrication company located in Springdale, Arkansas, providing services to the Northwest Arkansas area and beyond.

We specialize in stainless steel and rigging for industrial, commercial, and residential projects. With over 30 years of combined experience in the metal fabrication industry, our company has been built on integrity, quality, and reliability.

Our skilled craftsmen are equipped to handle both small and large jobs. We primarily serve processing and manufacturing plants, and in addition to welding we offer machining services for repair items and small to medium run parts.
