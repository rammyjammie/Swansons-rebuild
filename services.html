Services

Welding, fabrication, machining, and installation — handled start to finish by one shop in Springdale.

Core services

What We Build

We specialize in stainless steel and rigging for industrial, commercial, and residential projects — with the design help and licensed engineering to back it up.

Stainless Fabrication

Custom stainless fabrication and installation for processing and manufacturing plants — including complete systems and assembly built to your specifications.

Rigging & Installation

Equipment set, moved, and installed on site. We handle rigging for industrial, commercial, and residential jobs of all sizes.

Repair Work

Custom metal item repairs and machining for repair items, with the turnaround plants need to get a line back up and running.

Design Assistance

Concept designs, fabrication and production drawings, and 3D parametric modeling for custom projects — supported by licensed engineering.

Machining

In-house machining for small and medium run parts, replacement components, and pieces that are no longer easy to source.

Portable Welding

On-site welding for work that can't come to the shop — stainless, aluminum, and carbon steel handled in the field.
